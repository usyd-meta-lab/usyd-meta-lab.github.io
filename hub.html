All resources

Tools

  • Meta Measures Web app that calculates metacognition measures such as meta-d′ from your data, without writing the code yourself.
  • Forma Make clean diagrams of your study procedure for the method section of your thesis or paper.
  • Psych Norms Build word-pair lists for experiments, or look up the properties of word pairs you already have.
  • Meta-analysis in R Workshop materials on running a meta-analysis in R.
  • PIS Updater Upload a participant information sheet and it adds you as an investigator and updates the version and date. Run every PIS through this before using it.

Accounts & platforms

  • R The free statistics language the lab uses for all analysis. Install it before RStudio.
  • RStudio The program you write and run your R code in. Install R first, then RStudio.
  • JASP Free point-and-click statistics program, used alongside R for thesis analyses.
  • AsPredicted Preregister your hypotheses and analysis plan before you collect any data.
  • OSF The Open Science Framework. Share your cleaned data, analysis scripts and materials publicly.
  • myResearch Ethics The University system for submitting and managing human ethics applications.
  • OneDrive Syncs the shared lab folders. Share documents through here rather than by email.

Lab pages

  • Ethics The lab’s umbrella ethics applications and all their documents.
  • Honours This year’s Honours project themes, a roadmap for first semester, and how to write and present the proposal.
  • Recruitment Where to find participants: SONA, Prolific and the room booker for in-person testing.
  • Website The lab’s public website: people, research and news.
  • Publications Every lab paper, kept up to date from ORCID.
  • Meetings Lab meeting schedule and booking.

ESM tools

  • MetaForge An older platform that is now only used to code experience-sampling (ESM) studies.
  • MetaForge Live (iOS) iPhone app for experience-sampling (ESM) studies, where participants answer short surveys on their phone throughout the day. Supports images, video and complex visual displays.
  • ESM Dashboard Watch data come in while an experience-sampling study is running, so you can spot problems early.
  • ESM Scheduler Plan when participants in an experience-sampling study get their prompts across the day.
