Where do you think you sit?

First you'll place yourself on a bell curve for each of the Big Five personality traits. Then you'll complete the Mini-IPIP, a 20-item personality measure. Finally you'll see how your self-estimate compares to your measured score — and to the rest of the class, live.

Self-estimate vs. measured

A positive gap means you placed yourself higher than the questionnaire did. Watch what the class average does — the "better-than-average effect" is one of the most reliable findings in social psychology.

Percentiles for the Mini-IPIP are calculated against indicative published means and standard deviations from undergraduate samples (Donnellan et al., 2006), so they are approximate. Your rank within this class is calculated directly from the responses shown. A 20-item screener is far too short for any individual decision — it is used here purely to make the distribution visible.
